Vollständige Beschreibung

UniBE is interconnected. With us, you're part of an international community and benefit from interdisciplinary collaboration.

Your qualifications

  • A specialization in architectural history, ideally before 1900
  • Willingness to teach architectural history across a wide thematic and time scope (e.g. introduction to architectural history)
  • A willingness to develop a habilitation project (or second book) in architectural history, architectural theory, or monument preservation
  • Willingness to organize academic events and field trips and take on administrative tasks
  • Enthusiasm for working collaboratively in a collegial, international environment
  • A doctorate (or PhD) in art history, architectural history or architecture, completed by summer 2027 at the latest
  • Excellent written and spoken German; ideally a good command of English and French
  • The ability to work effectively as part of a team
  • Residence in Switzerland or a willingness to relocate to Switzerland; during the semester, a physical presence of 80% in the Bern office is required

We offer

  • A fully funded three-year postdoctoral position
  • The opportunity to pursue an innovative, independent research project in architectural history
  • An inspiring working environment at the Institute of Art History
  • Access to national and international research networks, archives, workshops, conferences and professional development opportunities
  • Opportunities to further develop skills in teaching and research methods as well as academic writing and publishing
  • Employment conditions and salary in accordance with University of Bern guidelines
  • A high quality of life in the Cantone of Bern, with a wide range of cultural and leisure activities (swimming in the River Aare, hiking and winter sports)

We are looking for

We are seeking a candidate who is nearing completion of their doctorate or already holds a doctorate in art history, architectural history, or architecture. Applicants should specialize in the history and theory of architecture, Baukultur or monument preservation (e.g. Europe, Southern/Eastern Europe, Latin America or the MENA region), ideally before 1900. This specialization should complement and broaden the Department's research and teaching. Responsibilities include developing an independent habilitation or second book project (50%), teaching at BA level (2–4 teaching hours per week during the semester), participating in the Department's academic activities (field trips, conferences and publications), and administrative duties. The working languages are German and English; excellent written and spoken German is required, especially for teaching. The appointment will be made at Early Postdoc level (a maximum net academic age of three years since the doctorate) or Advanced Postdoc level (a maximum net academic age of six years since the doctorate), at an employment level of 80-100%. The initial appointment is for three years and may be extended for a further three years following a successful evaluation. Net academic age is calculated in accordance with the SNSF guidelines; remuneration is governed by the University of Bern's Postdoc Regulations. Your qualifications

Brauche ich eine Grenzgänger-Bewilligung für eine Stelle im Kanton Bern?

EU/EFTA-Bürger mit Wohnsitz in einem EU/EFTA-Staat können eine G-Bewilligung beantragen und in der ganzen Schweiz arbeiten: Grenzzonen gelten für sie nicht mehr, und sie müssen mindestens einmal pro Woche heimkehren. Der Schweizer Arbeitgeber reicht den Antrag nach Vertragsunterzeichnung beim Migrationsamt des Kantons Bern ein. Die Bearbeitungszeiten variieren je nach Kanton — klären Sie die Details mit dem Migrationsamt des Kantons Bern oder mit HR während der Bewerbung.
