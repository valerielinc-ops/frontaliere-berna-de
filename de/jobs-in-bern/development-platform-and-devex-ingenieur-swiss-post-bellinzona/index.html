Wichtige Anforderungen

  • You have solid experience in software engineering with JavaScript/TypeScript and Node.js.
  • Additionally, you bring practical enterprise experience with Java or C# and contribute your expertise effectively.
  • You have several years of hands-on software engineering experience and design pragmatic, maintainable solutions.
  • Furthermore, you ideally have experience with developer platforms, Backstage, or similar portal and platform concepts, which helps you convince us with your expertise.
  • You are familiar with GitHub, GitHub Actions, CI/CD concepts, and DevOps principles and enjoy working across teams.
  • Moreover, you have English skills at level B2 and good knowledge of at least one Swiss national language, allowing you to communicate effectively and contribute successfully

Bewerbungsprozess

  • Additionally, you create application skeletons and templates that enable development teams to start new applications efficiently and bring in your expertise effectively.
  • You build developer-facing tools and workflows using JavaScript/TypeScript, Node.js, and modern web technologies.
  • Furthermore, you integrate existing application stacks, particularly in Java and/or C# environments, and apply your know-how successfully.
  • You develop and maintain tailor-made GitHub Actions for our CI/CD environment and automate build, test, and deployment processes.
  • Moreover, you collaborate closely with software engineering, DevOps, and platform teams and support them with questions related to platforms, tooling, and software delivery, allowing you to make a meaningful contribution.
  • You monitor the developer experience continuously and implement improvements where needed.
